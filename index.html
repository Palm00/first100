HOW IT WORKS

Four steps to get connected.

01

Reach out

Call, text, or fill out the form. Tell us the language you're most comfortable in.

02

Get matched

We pair you with a trained student navigator who speaks your language or works with an interpreter.

03

Get connected

Your navigator helps you reach real local resources — legal aid, schools, English classes, healthcare, and more.

04

Stay supported

Your navigator checks in through your first 100 days, adjusting the plan as things come up.

Legal & Immigration

Free help with paperwork, know-your-rights info, and court support. None of these charge by the hour.

Warning: in the U.S., a "notario" or "notario público" is NOT a lawyer and cannot give legal advice, sign your forms, or represent you in court — even if that's what the title means in your home country. Always confirm you're speaking with a licensed attorney or a DOJ-accredited representative before paying anyone for immigration help.

OUR PROMISE

You don't have to explain your status to get help.

Navigators are trained volunteers, not immigration officials. This program does not ask for or require proof of immigration status. If you're ever unsure about a call, link, or organization, ask your navigator first.

In an emergency, always call 911.
